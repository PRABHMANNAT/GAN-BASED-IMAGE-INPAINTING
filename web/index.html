A quick restoration guide

  1. Choose your image. The app processes a working copy up to 1,200 pixels wide or high.
  2. Mark the damage. Use brush, rectangle, or automatic scratch suggestions. Erase areas you want to keep.
  3. Restore. AI repair uses pretrained MI-GAN for natural scenes. Fast repair fills from nearby pixels. The separate edge-guided GAN is a small teaching model.
  4. Compare and export. Move the slider, inspect the details, and download your candidate and its processing record.

Keyboard: B brush · E eraser · R rectangle · H pan · [ / ] brush size · Ctrl/Cmd+Z undo · Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z redo · Enter restore. Import mask uses white for regions to repair.

Automatic detection can select legitimate image details. Review its suggestions. Neither GAN reproduces the trained model in the supplied project report. MI-GAN is pretrained by Picsart AI Research on Places2; the teaching model uses synthetic illustrations.
