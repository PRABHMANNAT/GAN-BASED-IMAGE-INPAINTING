Using Reframe

  1. Open an image. JPEG, PNG or WebP; a working copy is resized to a maximum of 1,200 pixels on its longest side.
  2. Select damage. Use the brush, rectangle or lasso. Hold Shift with the brush to draw a straight line. Use the eraser to subtract.
  3. Repair and review. Select a method, repair the area, then compare before and after. Apply the result to continue with another repair.
  4. Save your work. A .reframe project stores the image, selection, candidate and repair steps locally. Export the final image separately.

Keyboard shortcuts

B brush · E eraser · R rectangle · L lasso · H pan · [ / ] brush size · Ctrl/Cmd+Z undo selection · Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z redo · Space + drag pan · Enter repair · Escape cancel processing.
